# Icon brief

One icon per resource, drawn from the `iconConcept` below. Rules: the silhouette must read at **32 px** on a dark background; no two resources share a silhouette idea; fill with the category colour, add the accent as one small highlight or edge; no text, no emojis in the art. Colour family per category: 

- **Raw materials**: #D9A441
- **Processed goods**: #5CC8B0
- **Alloys**: #8FB4E8
- **Components**: #B28CF5
- **Energy**: #FF7A55
